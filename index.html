01 / OUR STORY · EST. 2024

More than
the miles.

Born in Khobar in 2024, Saturday Run Club started as a passion project and grew into a community built on genuine connection, wellness and personal progress.

Our mission is simple: create a safe, welcoming space for people to connect, grow and enjoy running together.
